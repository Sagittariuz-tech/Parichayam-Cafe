# UI context
## Theme
A coastal café menu styled like a printed food journal with editorial photography and warm side light.
## Colors
Parchment #f6f1e7, deep forest #1b3026, rust #a3462d, muted ink #5e5e4f, sand #d8d1c2.
## Typography
Instrument Serif for display and DM Sans for reading, labels, and prices.
## Layout
Split feature on desktop, image beneath headline on phone. Horizontally scrollable featured cards and quick categories. Full menu with search, dietary filters, and a native category drawer. Keep buttons at least 44px tall.

## Update
The supplied café mark appears in the header and footer as an SVG. On phones, the highlights show one full card per snap and provide swipe and arrow controls. Heading letters reveal as sections enter view. Animated staff portraits welcome guests once per session and acknowledge successful orders.

## Dish and category refinement
Each dish opens with its fully visible photograph, editorial description, and Order it action. The order step uses a table selector, quantity stepper, and three optional kitchen-note chips tailored to the item. Category rows have small WebP photographs, and the drawer has independent scrolling. The browser tab uses a warm, high-contrast variant of the site's SVG mark.
